# Thor’s Hammer

**Beginner Version — 5 Rounds for Time**

## Overview
- **Format:** 5 rounds, complete all movements in order
- **Goal:** Finish as fast as possible **with good form**
- **Rest:** As needed to maintain safe technique
- **Optional time cap:** 25–30 minutes
- **Equipment:** 1 kettlebell

> **Notation:** “/side” means **do the reps on each side** (e.g., 5/side = 5 left + 5 right).

## Workout (5 Rounds)
1. 5 Kettlebell Dead Cleans / side
2. 10 Kettlebell Swings
3. 5 Overhead Presses / side
4. 10 Kettlebell Goblet Squats
5. 10 Goblet Reverse Lunges (Left)
6. 10 Goblet Reverse Lunges (Right)
7. 10 One-Arm Rows (Right)
8. 10 One-Arm Rows (Left)
9. 10 Push-Ups
10. 10 Burpees

## Suggested scaling (beginner)
- **Swings:** reduce to 5–8 reps, or do kettlebell deadlifts if hinge mechanics aren’t solid yet.
- **Presses:** use a lighter bell; reduce to 3/side if form breaks.
- **Push-ups:** incline push-ups (hands on a bench/box).
- **Burpees:** step back/step up; remove the push-up.

## Safety notes
- Stop a set early if your back position breaks down (especially on swings/cleans).
- Keep the kettlebell close on cleans; avoid banging the forearm.
- If shoulders feel unstable overhead, reduce range or swap presses for front rack holds.
