# 🏋️ 24-Minute Kettlebell Workout

[Source Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eGEJgbwv11w)

---

## 🤸 Warm-Up
* **Format:** 40 seconds work | 5 seconds rest
* [0:17 - Warm-Up Introduction](https://youtube.com&t=17s)

### Exercises
* [0:23](https://youtube.com&t=23s) **Jumping Jacks**
* [1:13](https://youtube.com&t=73s) **Cossack Squats**
* [1:58](https://youtube.com&t=118s) **Cat-Cows**
* [2:40](https://youtube.com&t=160s) **Downward Dog to Cobra**
* [3:24](https://youtube.com&t=204s) **Frankenstein Kicks**
* [4:10](https://youtube.com&t=250s) **Toe Touches**

---

## 😇 Block 1: The "Cody" Complex (The Angel)
* **Format:** 4 sets total [[0:53]](https://youtube.com&t=53s)
* **Instructions:** Complete the left side, immediately switch to the right side, and finish the block with Cossack squats [[5:28]](https://youtube.com&t=328s).

### Left Side
* [5:08](https://youtube.com&t=308s) **5 One-Arm Kettlebell Rows**
* [5:13](https://youtube.com&t=313s) **4 One-Arm Kettlebell Swings**
* [5:13](https://youtube.com&t=313s) **3 One-Arm Kettlebell Snatches**

### Right Side
* [5:28](https://youtube.com&t=328s) **Repeat Sequence:** 5 Rows, 4 Swings, 3 Snatches

### Finisher
* [5:44](https://youtube.com&t=344s) **5 Cossack Squats** *(Per side, holding kettlebell)*

---

## 😈 Block 2: The "Devil's Tricycle" Complex (The Devil)
* **Format:** 4 sets total [[17:23]](https://youtube.com&t=1043s)
* **Instructions:** Complete the three kettlebell movements consecutively, then immediately drop to the floor for push-ups [[17:09]](https://youtube.com&t=1029s).

### Movement Sequence
* [15:40](https://youtube.com&t=940s) **6 Two-Handed Kettlebell Swings**
* [15:48](https://youtube.com&t=948s) **6 Kettlebell Deadlift High Pulls**
* [16:06](https://youtube.com&t=966s) **3 Single-Arm Squat Cleans** *(Left side)*
* [16:06](https://youtube.com&t=966s) **3 Single-Arm Squat Cleans** *(Right side)*
* [16:46](https://youtube.com&t=1006s) **Diamond Push-Ups** *(Max repetitions to failure)*
